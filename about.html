An archive of progress.

Come What May Tomorrow is a song about moving forward without knowing exactly what tomorrow will bring. This archive documents the song as it changed from an early instrumental idea into the finished recording.

Rather than presenting only the final version, the website lets you move through five stages of the song and hear how its instrumentation, vocals, recordings and mix developed over time. The process itself becomes part of the finished piece.

This connects to the theme of Upkeep: things we make are rarely finished once and left untouched. They are maintained, revised, repaired and cared for. Here, upkeep becomes musical — each version is a record of attention, change and the small decisions that gradually shape a song.

PROCESS

Project development log

01

Website structure

Established the HTML, CSS and JavaScript framework for the interactive song archive, including the player, timeline, waveform, supporting information and footer.

02

Song versions

Added five audio stages documenting the song's development from the original instrumental through to the final mix.

03

Interactive timeline

Developed the timeline slider so the audience can move through the song's different stages and hear the progression between versions.

04

Audio playback

Built Web Audio API playback that blends the five versions according to the timeline position, making the transitions between stages part of the interaction.

05

Waveform visualisation

Added a live waveform to create a visual relationship between the audio and the interface while the song is playing.

06

Floating imagery

Added project PNGs around the player and developed randomised, organic movement so the visual elements feel connected to the music without competing with the controls.

07

Responsive refinement

Adjusted the player, timeline, typography and floating imagery for smaller screens and different viewport sizes.

08

Visual refinement

Iteratively refined spacing, typography, timeline styling, filled stage points, shadows and page hierarchy to create a more minimal and cohesive interface.

09

Audio initialisation fix

Removed obsolete movement-function calls left over from an earlier animation system, restoring the audio graph and allowing the playback interface to initialise correctly.

10

Archive structure

Separated the project explanation and development history from the main player, creating a dedicated About page so the home experience can remain focused on listening and interaction.
